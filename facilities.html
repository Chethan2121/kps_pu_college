Facilities

A safe, modern and student-centric campus environment.

KPS PU College provides thoughtfully designed infrastructure and facilities that support focused learning, healthy living and balanced growth.

Separate Hostel Facilities

Comfortable, safe and supervised hostel accommodation with dedicated wardens, nutritious food and structured study hours.

  • Separate hostels for boys and girls.
  • 24/7 security and CCTV monitoring (where applicable).
  • Study hall with faculty support during key hours.

Library & Reading Room

A well-stocked library with textbooks, reference material, competitive exam guides and periodicals.

  • Calm reading spaces for focused study.
  • Dedicated reference section for NEET, JEE & CET.
  • Access to journals and curated learning resources.

Physics Laboratory

A dynamic Physics lab designed to explore the fundamental laws of nature through practical experiments, measurements, and real-world applications.

  • Hands-on experiments in mechanics, optics, and electricity.
  • Use of precision instruments for accurate measurements.
  • Encourages analytical thinking and scientific observation.

Chemistry Laboratory

A modern Chemistry lab that enables students to understand chemical reactions, compounds, and processes through safe and guided experimentation.

  • Practical sessions covering organic and inorganic chemistry.
  • Safe handling and storage of chemicals.
  • Focus on accuracy in titration and analysis techniques.

Biology Laboratory

A well-equipped Biology lab that provides insight into living organisms through observation, dissection, and microscopic studies.

  • Microscopic study of cells, tissues, and specimens.
  • Hands-on learning in botany and zoology experiments.
  • Enhances understanding of biological processes and systems.

Computer Laboratory

A fully equipped Computer lab that empowers students with digital skills, programming knowledge, and exposure to modern technologies.

  • Access to modern systems with updated software.
  • Programming practice in multiple languages.
  • Introduction to real-world applications and digital tools.

Digital Classrooms

Spacious, well-ventilated and well-lit classrooms with digital teaching aids for engaging sessions.

  • Smart boards / projectors where applicable.
  • Comfortable seating for long study hours.
  • Structured seating to facilitate individual focus.

Sports & Wellness

Physical fitness and recreation through indoor and outdoor sports, events and wellness activities.

  • Encouragement to participate in games and events.
  • Helps maintain a healthy balance between study and recreation.

Campus Infrastructure

Modern infrastructure designed to support academic excellence, comfort and overall student development.

  • Spacious classrooms with smart teaching aids.
  • Well-equipped laboratories and libraries.
  • Clean campus with dedicated study and activity zones.
